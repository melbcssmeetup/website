Melbourne CSS

A community in Melbourne that holds meetups to share CSS knowledge! Often over pizza & drinks.

Anyone interested in CSS is welcome to join, whether you're a complete beginner or seasoned expert. Meetups typically take place on the first Tuesday of every second month.
